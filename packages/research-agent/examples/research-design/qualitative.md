# Qualitative Research Design Example

Topic: municipal oversight of algorithm vendors.

- Question: How do different procurement arrangements shape municipal oversight practices?
- Claim mode: comparative.
- Concepts: procurement arrangement, oversight authority, information access, escalation, and sanctions.
- Method decision: structured focused comparison is selected over a single-case study to compare declared oversight mechanisms; small purposive samples limit generalization.
- Case selection: choose contrasting procurement forms using stated criteria and document contextual differences.
- Materials: public contracts, oversight records, and consented key-informant interviews.
- Interview plan: use role-specific guides, record consent and nonresponse, and protect employment identity.
- Analysis: ask common questions across cases, retain negative evidence, and preserve within-case and cross-case evidence matrices with locators.
- Alternatives: municipal legal capacity and leadership priorities may explain observed differences.
- Boundary: only procurements with observable oversight records are included.

The protocol records selection logic and audit requirements. It does not treat interview accounts as population frequencies, automatically infer causality, or claim that an ethics checklist is approval.
