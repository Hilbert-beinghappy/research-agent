# Quantitative Research Design Example

Topic: algorithmic explanations and institutional trust in municipal services.

- Question: How is explanation quality associated with institutional trust among residents receiving automated decisions?
- Claim mode: associational, not causal.
- Concepts: explanation quality (exposure); institutional trust (outcome); prior service satisfaction (alternative explanation).
- Method decision: a three-wave panel survey is selected over a cross-sectional survey because temporal ordering and attrition can be observed. It still cannot eliminate time-varying confounding.
- Sampling: recruit from documented service-user frames and report nonresponse and attrition by wave.
- Measurement: freeze explanation-quality and trust scales before outcome analysis.
- Analysis: estimate within-person associations with declared controls and robustness checks.
- Preanalysis: freeze variables, exclusions, models, uncertainty calculation, and robustness checks.
- Boundary: findings apply only to sampled resident-facing municipal services.
- Ethics: obtain consent and minimize linkage fields.

The corresponding question, concepts, relation, critical method decision, and protocol are separate confirmed records. Each retains Operation provenance and an explicit evidence gap until canonical Evidence/Claim records support the choice.
