# Evidence Rules

Use the project's recorded material state as the ceiling for every statement.

| Recorded level | Permitted use | Required wording or limit |
|---|---|---|
| `metadata` | Discovery and bibliographic description | Do not attribute findings, methods, or conclusions. |
| `abstract` | What the abstract explicitly reports | Say “the abstract reports/claims”; do not call it a full-text finding. |
| `fulltext_unlocated` | Document availability and broad reading status | Do not quote or attach a page-specific claim. |
| `fulltext_located` | A claim supported by stable page/section/anchor evidence | Keep the locator and relation visible in working notes. |
| `table_or_figure_located` | Values or patterns in a located table or figure | Name the table/figure and do not infer measures not shown. |
| `dataset_or_appendix_located` | Located data or appendix material | State whether the support comes from data, code, or supplementary text. |

Citation verification is independent of evidence support. A real DOI does not prove a claim; a located passage does not prove that citation metadata or publication status is current.

For exact quotations, require an exact-match excerpt allowed by the recorded rights. Otherwise paraphrase and retain the locator. Never reconstruct missing wording.

Treat `supports`, `qualifies`, and `contradicts` as distinct. A mixed claim stays mixed until the conflict is resolved or its boundary conditions are stated. “Insufficient” is a valid result and must not be upgraded by confident prose.

Do not export restricted abstracts or excerpts. Let `research_artifacts` apply rights filtering, input hashes, citation gates, overwrite approval, and submission confirmation.
