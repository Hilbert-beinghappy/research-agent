# Management and Public Administration Review Lens

## Scope before comparison

Keep these dimensions explicit because apparent disagreement often reflects a change in level or context:

- individual, team, organization, inter-organizational network, policy, or institutional level;
- public, private, nonprofit, hybrid, or platform organization;
- country, jurisdiction, administrative tradition, policy field, and implementation setting;
- citizen, street-level worker, manager, agency, vendor, or regulated actor;
- descriptive, explanatory, causal, interpretive, design, or normative question;
- construct definition, measure, intervention, mechanism, outcome, and time horizon.

## Search concepts

Build query blocks for the focal phenomenon, actor or setting, mechanism or theory, and outcome. Keep genuine synonyms together but do not equate adjacent constructs such as transparency, explainability, accountability, trust, legitimacy, adoption, and compliance. Record Chinese and English terms when both are relevant, and report source-language and database coverage limits.

## Synthesis dimensions

Compare studies by theory, construct operationalization, unit of analysis, design, sample or cases, institutional context, mechanism, outcome, and boundary condition. Separate normative arguments from empirical estimates and observational associations from causal effects.

Do not transport a private-sector result to public administration, a citizen response to employee behavior, or one jurisdiction to another without a stated reason and limitation. Do not infer implementation effects from attitudes alone.

## Research gaps

Classify a gap as a recorded coverage gap, evidence gap, construct or measurement conflict, context gap, method gap, mechanism gap, or temporal gap. A gap must follow from the search log and evidence matrix. Use “the recorded search did not identify…” when coverage cannot establish absence from the field.
