# AI disclosure template

Use this as input to `research_manuscript` with `action: "create_disclosure"`. The user must inspect and confirm the final record.

```text
AI use:
- [Describe ideation, search planning, drafting, translation, code assistance, or rubric review.]

Models:
- [Exact model IDs used, or "none".]

Human responsibilities:
- The authors verified every factual statement, source, quotation, locator, analysis result, and citation.
- The authors made all design, interpretation, authorship, and submission decisions.

Limitations:
- AI output may be incomplete or wrong and is not human peer review.
- [Add project-specific limitations.]

Decisions not automated:
- Evidence acceptance, coding decisions, causal interpretation, review dispositions, and submission approval.
```

Do not claim that a model verified its own output. Follow the target journal, institution, funder, and applicable law when they require a different or additional disclosure.
